Read in another language, one held word at a time.

Pagegloss is an iPad reader for PDFs, ebooks and pictures of pages. Touch and hold a word to see what it means in its own sentence, then save it, highlight it or add a note.

  • PDF, EPUB and AZW3, plus photos and scans of pages, whose text Pagegloss reads on your iPad.
  • Translation on your iPad with Apple's languages, or an online service you choose with your own key.
  • Highlights and notes, typed or written with Apple Pencil, kept with your book.
  • Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your documents stay on your iPad.

Pagegloss is for iPad.
