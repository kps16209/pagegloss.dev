Five things a dictionary app can't do.

Pagegloss reads the sentence around your word, works on pages other readers can't open, remembers the meanings you choose, and keeps your study in one place.

01 · Meaning in context

Hold a word: the meaning in this sentence

Not a list of every sense. The panel leads with the one this sentence uses, with its pronunciation, even on a scanned page.

01 · Meaning in context

The whole sentence, explained

One tap translates the sentence. On supported iPads, Apple Intelligence explains what it means, on the iPad.

02 · Scans and photos

Pages other readers can't use

A photocopied chapter, a phone photo of a page, a scanned PDF: Pagegloss reads the text on your iPad. Hold a word, drag a box around a few lines to translate or explain them, or tap a web address or phone number in a picture.

03 · Your terms

Choose a meaning once, for the whole document

In an economics reading, "mature" means "falls due". Pin that meaning and every later lookup leads with it; Translate page and AI services follow it. Your terms list exports to Anki or a spreadsheet, and can be underlined on the page.

04 · Bilingual page

The page in your language, under it

Open the bilingual view and the whole page appears translated below the original, or beside it. Tap a paragraph and it lights up on both sides, so you never lose your place.

05 · A place to study

Search everything, quote it properly

Search the text of every document on your iPad; a result opens the page with the words marked. Copy Quote adds the page in Plain, APA, MLA, Chicago or Harvard style. Saved words come back in a short daily review, and a study sheet prints them with their sentences.
